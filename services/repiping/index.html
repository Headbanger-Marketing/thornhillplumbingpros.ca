Questions About the Work

Is the repiping question about one section or every floor?

Symptoms on one floor may point to a single section, while the same symptom in every bathroom and kitchen may call for a wider assessment.

  • Identify the pipe material by room

    Ask the respondent to establish what the visible supply pipes are made of and which rooms they feed, starting from the main shutoff. Photograph the pipe where it is visible near the meter, the heater or under sinks, and mark those spots on your sketch. Do not cut, open or disconnect anything to look further; that is left to the provider.

  • Pipes for the marked rooms or the whole run

    Compare replacing the one section that serves the affected rooms with replacing the whole run, and ask whether the rooms marked on your sketch favour one over the other. Ask which walls or ceilings would need openings and what restoration is proposed.

  • Record which lines feed which rooms

    If repiping is agreed for your Thornhill home, ask for a written record of which lines were replaced, which rooms and floors they feed, and where the new shutoffs sit. Add the finished route to your sketch.
