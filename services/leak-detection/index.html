Questions About the Work

Is the leak directly above the stain or somewhere else?

Water can travel along a joist or pipe before it shows, so a stain may sit away from its source; a sketch of the rooms around it helps.

  • Place each stain against the rooms above

    Before any checking method is chosen, the respondent should know where each stain sits on your sketch and what lies directly above it, whether a bathroom, a kitchen or an outside wall. Note which fixtures were running when it appeared and whether it changes after rain or only after use. Say whether the stain in your Thornhill home has grown, and photograph it with a reference object.

  • Compare a visible source with a hidden one

    Compare a drip at a visible fitting with moisture that appears with no visible source, and ask what non-invasive checks the respondent proposes before any opening is made. Ask who restores the finish and how drying will be confirmed.

  • Record the source and the affected rooms

    If a source is found and repaired, ask for a written note naming where it was, which rooms on your sketch it touched and what was done to it. Moisture noticed elsewhere, such as a second stain or a damp wall, should be listed as a separate item with its own proposed scope.
